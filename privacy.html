    What this notice covers

    This notice covers the dominusos.ai website: this page, the home page and the white paper page. Dominus Foundry LLC publishes the site and is responsible for it. The site has no forms and no accounts. You can read every page without giving us anything.

    Analytics: only if you allow it

    The site can use two analytics services, and only after you choose “Allow analytics”. Nothing is sent until you choose. If you choose “No analytics”, or make no choice, neither service is loaded.

    • Google Analytics (Google LLC). If you allow it, your browser loads Google’s script and sends page views. These include the page address without any query string or fragment, the page title, and the previous page’s address without its query string. Google sets the cookies _ga and _ga_Z0BTF1J29X to recognise a returning browser. Google also receives your IP address, as any website you contact does.
    • Vercel Web Analytics. If you allow it, your browser sends page views to an address on this site. They also carry no query string or fragment. It sets no cookie.

    We do not use advertising tags, we do not sell information about visitors, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

    Your analytics choice

    Your browser has JavaScript turned off, so this site loads no analytics at all.

    You can change your mind here at any time. Choosing “No analytics” after allowing it stops both services. It also clears the Google Analytics cookies this site can clear, and reloads the page so no script that had already loaded keeps running. Your choice is kept in this browser only, in two places: a cookie and a copy in browser storage. Both are honored for 180 days from when you chose, and the site reads the storage copy first. After 180 days, your choice expires and is treated as if you had not chosen: analytics stays off and you are asked again. Because the storage copy stays valid for those same 180 days, clearing your cookies alone does not bring the question back before then.

    Global Privacy Control

    If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as “No analytics”. You are not asked, and the signal overrides an earlier “Allow” for as long as it is on.

    The site reads the signal the way your browser offers it to the page (navigator.globalPrivacyControl). That part is tested.

    The site is also set up to answer the Sec-GPC request header: when a page request carries it, our hosting is configured to set a short-lived consent_gpc cookie that the page treats the same way. That part has not yet been confirmed on the live site. If your browser sends only the header and not the page signal, choose “No analytics” above to be sure.

    What is stored in your browser

    • dos-consent: your analytics choice. It is always set once you choose, because without it we could not respect the choice. Both the cookie and the browser-storage copy are honored for 180 days from when you chose, then the choice expires and you are asked again.
    • consent_gpc (cookie, one day): meant to be set when your browser sends a Global Privacy Control header and cleared when it stops (not yet confirmed on the live site, see above). It means “No analytics”.
    • dos-theme (browser storage): light or dark theme, if you switch it.
    • _ga and _ga_Z0BTF1J29X: only if you allow analytics (see above). Google sets them to last up to two years unless you clear them or choose “No analytics”.

    Other services your browser contacts

    Every page also loads two things from other companies. They load whatever you choose about analytics, and with JavaScript off too. They are not analytics, and we receive nothing from them about you. The companies receive your IP address and the name of this site, as any server you contact does.

    • Typefaces from Google Fonts (Google LLC).
    • The verification badge in the page footer, an image served by LocateFaith.

    Hosting

    Our hosting provider serves the site and keeps standard request logs: IP address, browser type, the address requested and the time. Those logs are kept for operating and securing the site, not for analytics.

    If you contact us

    The site lists an email address and a phone number. If you write or call, we receive what you send and use it to reply. Calls to (505) 520-1433 may be recorded, and you are told at the start of the call. For Dominus Foundry’s other services, see dominusfoundry.com/privacy.

    How long we keep it

    We have not set a fixed deletion date for analytics or for messages you send us. Ask us and we will delete what we hold about you.
